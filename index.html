Who's behind the keys

Not a guru. A guy taking notes.

I'm Shaah. Twenty years in systems engineering and architecture, a hip hop producer out of the DMV, a visual artist, and lately a student of money and property. Between Keys is where those worlds meet.

This isn't expert advice or coaching. It's what I'm learning, as I learn it. When I don't know something, I ask the people who do: realtors, lenders, financial advisors, artists, builders. Then I bring the answers back here.

The goal is simple. Make better moves with money, and bring people along for the ride toward a more stable future. Especially artists, creatives, and anyone who's been told this stuff isn't for them.

Every key opens something. The trick is finding out which door it fits, and sharing the copy.

How this works

The ground rules.

  • Questions out loudI bring the questions my family, friends, and community are asking to people who answer them for a living, and turn what I learn into posts.
  • Key Takeaways, every timeEvery piece ends with the short version. If you only read one part, read that.
  • Built with AI, directed by peopleBetween Keys is co-created with Claude. When Claude does the research, I say so. I direct it, check it, and sign off on it.
  • Not adviceI'm sharing what I'm learning, not telling you what to do with your money. Talk to a licensed pro before you make a big move.
