Who's behind the keys

Not a guru. A guy taking notes.

I'm Shaah. Twenty years in systems engineering and architecture, even more making music as a hobbyist, and lately a visual artist and a student of money and property. Between Keys is where those worlds meet.

This isn't expert advice or coaching. It's what I'm learning, as I learn it. When I don't know something, I ask the people who do: realtors, lenders, financial advisors, artists, builders. Then I bring the answers back here so we can learn it together.

The goal is simple. Make better moves with money, and bring family and community along toward a more stable future. Especially artists, creatives, and anyone who's been told this stuff isn't for them.

Every key has a purpose. Let's learn what we can unlock.

How this works

The ground rules.

  • Questions out loudI bring the questions our families, friends, and community are asking to the people who answer them for a living, and turn what we learn into posts.
  • Key TakeawaysWhen a piece calls for it, it ends with the short version. If you only read one part, read that.
  • Built with AI, directed by peopleBetween Keys is co-created with my AI assistant. When it does the research, I say so. I direct it, check it, and sign off on it.
  • Not adviceI'm sharing what I'm learning, not telling you what to do with your money. Talk to a licensed pro before you make a big move.
